Description

Turn-Key 3-Bedroom House with Self-Contained Basement Apartment, Workshop & Garage.

A superb turn-key property set on approximately 0.53 hectares (1.31 acres) of beautifully landscaped grounds, located just 2km from Newport town.

Built in 2007 and fully completed in 2020, this well-presented property offers excellent versatility and additional accommodation, making it ideal as a family home or potential rental opportunity.

The main house comprises three bedrooms, with a one-bedroom self-contained apartment in the basement, providing valuable additional living space.

The property also benefits from a substantial workshop, incorporating a dedicated office and WC, making it particularly attractive to those working from home or requiring workshop/storage facilities.

Externally, the property features a landscaped garden and tarmac driveway, along with a garage providing secure parking and storage.

The home is equipped with a biomass boiler and 14 photovoltaic solar panels, offering energy-efficient heating and renewable electricity generation.
